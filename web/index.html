Dimension

Buildings rise by their real height in metres. Districts and the analysis layers rise by the value being mapped, so magnitude is read as height rather than only as colour.

Projection

The basemap supplies world context: coastlines, neighbouring countries and place names. Choosing None removes every third-party request and leaves only the boundaries published with this site.

About

Open spatial data for Ghana: 16 regions, 260 districts, buildings, roads, facilities and terrain, in one database with one set of conventions.

Everything shown here is queryable in SQL, downloadable as GeoParquet, and servable as vector tiles.

Reading the data

Distances are straight-line, not travel time. Flood exposure is derived from observed historical surface water and terrain; it is not a modelled flood risk and carries no return period. Population is modelled from census totals rather than counted.

Administrative units join on p-codes, not names: GH07 is Greater Accra and GH0701 a district within it.
